## Firewall summary

The current upstream record separates three different things that must not be collapsed:

1. **parent EPRL/KKL composition data** — E3 contraction/gluing, E4 face/edge weights/internal sums/normalization, E6 noncompact gauge redundancy/fixing;
2. **causal Toller-modified local/generalized vertices** — source-defined causal local objects;
3. **transport of supported K5 extension data through a physical causal many-vertex construction** — currently missing.

The existence of (1) and (2) does not establish their physical inheritance into (3).

## Required object for a future physical coherence gate

A future gate must pin an actual many-vertex/refinement object before asking what equation it induces on extension data. At minimum it must identify, from source or explicitly new principle:

- the domain and codomain boundary spaces;
- the exact two-/three-vertex or refinement complex;
- all integration/sum variables;
- face/edge measures and normalization;
- orientation and causal-label compatibility;
- gluing/contraction map and composition order;
- gauge quotient/fixing and its normalization;
- how a local distributional extension enters the composed object;
- whether differences supported on `N` are transported, annihilated, mixed, or generate new supported strata;
- how order-zero tangential coefficients and normal derivatives through order 8 transform.

Until these are pinned, no equation such as

```text
f(gh)=f(g)f(h)
```

is physical authority. CD002-A remains `CONDITIONAL_SELECTOR_POWER_ONLY`.

## SOURCE_DERIVATION_REQUIRED lock

Any future law used as a physical selector must be classified explicitly as either:

- `SOURCE_DERIVED`, or
- `NEW_PHYSICAL_PRINCIPLE`.

A law used only to measure mathematical selector power is `CONDITIONAL_SELECTOR_POWER_ONLY`. There is no admissible implicit category called “reasonable assumption.”
