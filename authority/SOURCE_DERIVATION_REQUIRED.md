# SOURCE_DERIVATION_REQUIRED

This is a mandatory authority firewall for every future CDSR selector law.

## Allowed statuses

Any law

```text
P[T]=0
```

used to make a **physical** selection claim must carry exactly one of:

### `SOURCE_DERIVED`

The law is derived from a pinned primary/source-authoritative causal composition, refinement, boundary-value, spectral, or other physical object. The derivation must specify the exact object, domain, assumptions, and how it acts on the relevant extension-space sector.

### `NEW_PHYSICAL_PRINCIPLE`

The law is not forced by the frozen source theory and is introduced explicitly as new model content. It must be prospectively motivated and preregistered before testing its success on the K5 ambiguity.

A law studied only to measure mathematical power must instead be labeled:

### `CONDITIONAL_SELECTOR_POWER_ONLY`

This status cannot support the inference `SOURCE IMPLIES P`. CD002-A is the current canonical example.

## Forbidden implicit category

There is no admissible status

```text
REASONABLE_ASSUMPTION
```

or “natural enough to use physically.” If a law is not source-derived, physical use requires declaring it new physical principle content.

## Promotion rule

A conditional theorem of the form

```text
IF P THEN uniqueness/reduction
```

may be combined with physics only after a separate authority establishes either

```text
SOURCE_DERIVED: source structure => P
```

or an explicit model decision establishes

```text
NEW_PHYSICAL_PRINCIPLE: postulate P
```

with prospective no-smuggling and falsifiability/consistency gates.

Without that transition, physical-selector claims remain false/unauthorized.
