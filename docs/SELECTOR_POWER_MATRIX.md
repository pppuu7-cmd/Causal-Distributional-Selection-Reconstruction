## Reading rules

1. `normalized multiplicativity` is not the preferred physical answer. Its row records a control of **selector power** only.
2. An `OPEN` source-derived law may turn out to be multiplicative, projective, cocyclic, weighted, functorial, affine, operator-valued, jet-valued, or something else. The source object decides.
3. A row can have strong mathematical power and still fail physical source authority or the no-smuggling audit.
4. Tangential shape, overall scalar, normal jets, and physical equivalence are separate columns because success in one does not imply success in the others.
5. No row currently has `Physical uniqueness established = YES`.

## Update contract

Future terminal results must update only the cells they actually establish. Unknown cells stay `OPEN`; do not infer downstream successes from a selector's action on one sector.
